# Astrogen Article Image Design System

## Stable Brand Layer

- Premium editorial realism or refined editorial abstraction.
- Soft neutral base, natural depth, polished but believable light.
- Burgundy/wine and warm gold are restrained accents, not mandatory wardrobe,
  walls, furniture, or props.
- Deep green, mint, or teal may appear when they support the topic.
- Esoteric meaning stays subtle and specific. No mystical clutter, neon-purple
  astrology, random zodiac wheels, fake glyphs, or rendered text.

## Variable Narrative Layer

Brand consistency does not mean repeating the same scene. Human covers vary on
nine recorded axes: scene archetype, setting, subject arrangement, action,
emotional beat, gaze, shot distance, camera angle, and dominant props. A new
direction must differ from every recent fingerprint on at least four axes.

Do not reuse the current overrepresented formula: seated person or couple at a
wooden table, bright window, laptop, notebook, cup or plant, burgundy sweater,
and a neutral thoughtful expression.

## Emotion

Every `human_scene` has one article-specific emotional beat. Useful beats
include uncertainty, curiosity, anticipation, discovery, recognition, relief,
resolve, reassurance, trust, tenderness, concern, connection, hope, and
controlled surprise.

`Calm`, `thoughtful`, `focused`, and `reflective` are tone modifiers, not
emotions. The selected beat needs at least two visible cues in the face, gaze,
posture, hands, movement, distance between people, or active interaction. The
emotion must remain readable at blog-card crop without becoming theatrical.

Do not turn every positive emotion into a smile. Relief may show in released
shoulders; recognition in changed gaze and posture; uncertainty in a paused
gesture; trust in attentive proximity; resolve in a deliberate movement.

## Scene Rotation

Use the scene that best communicates the article: a decision point, active
conversation, discovery moment, expert listening, family interaction,
relationship exchange, personal reflection, post-consultation relief, or a
transition in motion. Rotate close-up, medium, over-shoulder, and wide
environmental framing. Viewer-facing gaze is occasional and meaningful, with a
working target of one or two among the latest six human covers.

## Abstract Covers

Use `abstract_graphic` for definitions, systems, zodiac profiles, lists,
comparisons, frameworks, metrics, and non-personal explanations. Keep the same
premium light/material language while varying geometry, scale, negative space,
surface, movement, and symbolic logic. Abstract covers contain no people,
faces, hands, bodies, silhouettes, or model-like figures.

## Acceptance

- The topic is understandable within two seconds without title text.
- The declared emotion is actually visible, not merely claimed in QA notes.
- The concept passes company-scoped visual-history preflight.
- Human anatomy, hands, surfaces, lighting, and meaningful props are coherent.
- No text, pseudo-writing, UI, screen reflections, watermark, or stock-photo
  pose is visible.
- A technically clean but emotionally blank or template-like cover fails QA.
