# 15 Strategic Opportunity Brief

## Purpose

Reusable process contract for a strategic growth layer between Product Discovery and downstream execution.

Product Discovery answers: what is this product or route?

Strategic Opportunity answers: what growth role could it play, and what should a human approve before agents execute?

## Default Sequence

1. Stage 10 Product Discovery
2. Stage 15 Strategic Opportunity Brief
3. Human strategy decision
4. Downstream execution lanes only after approval

## Default Owner

- Manager: CMO
- Specialist: `MKT Growth Strategy Architect`

## Output Language

Use the resolved output language from the manager brief or canonical company defaults for human-facing artifacts and decision packets.

## When Required

Use Stage 15 when a product, route, feature, or offer may affect:

- acquisition;
- conversion;
- lead capture;
- monetization;
- product positioning;
- offer architecture;
- trust-building;
- lifecycle messaging;
- SEO/content strategy;
- paid or social campaign direction.

For free products and free entry routes, Stage 15 is required by default.

## In Scope

- lead-magnet, entry-product, upsell-bridge, and trust-builder hypotheses;
- funnel role options;
- monetization paths;
- audience and segment implications;
- offer/package strategy;
- lifecycle/email/chat capture ideas;
- channel implications at strategy level;
- risks and brand conflicts;
- human decision packet;
- downstream issue map after approval.

## Out Of Scope

- replacing Product Discovery;
- full competitor research;
- validated segment claims;
- semantic core generation;
- content plans;
- ad copy;
- article briefs;
- publication;
- implementation.

## Acceptance Gate

The manager may accept a Stage 15 artifact only if:

- accepted Product Discovery is cited;
- product facts and strategic hypotheses are clearly separated;
- free/entry routes explicitly consider lead magnet and funnel role;
- at least 3 strategic options are presented when plausible;
- risks and brand conflicts are visible;
- a human decision packet is included;
- no downstream execution is started automatically.
