Status: working v0.1

This is the source-tracked working map for Astrogen target-audience segments by product or product surface.

Important distinction: a target-audience segment is a group of people in a similar life, emotional, motivational, or decision state. Keyword clusters, GSC queries, GA4 behavior, CrawlObserver page data, and semantic-core inventory are evidence for segments, not the segments themselves.

## Cross-Product Audience States

These are not product pages by themselves, but they should guide content planning, onboarding, CTA choice, internal linking, and expert matching.

| Audience state | Typical product fit | What the person is really asking | Content / CTA implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-understanding after confusion or transition | `/horoscope`, `/solar`, `/experts`, Human Design, astrology experts | "What is happening with me, and how do I understand myself now?" | Use gentle educational content, low-pressure entry, and clear next-step choices. |
| Self-trust confirmation | natal chart products, `/money`, `/relationships`, `/solar`, `/horoscope`, astrology experts | "I think I already know what is right for me, but I need a calm external mirror before I trust my own decision." | Position astrology as a reflective confirmation tool, not an authority that replaces the person's agency. Use language such as "check in with yourself", "see your intuition in a wider frame", and "name what you already feel". |
| Relationship uncertainty | `/relationships`, `/experts/astrologiya`, `/experts/psykhologiya`, `/experts/taro` | "What is happening between us, and what should I do?" | Avoid deterministic compatibility claims; offer reflective framing and expert help. |
| Money/career pressure | `/money`, astrology experts, coaching | "Why do I repeat financial/work patterns, and where should I focus?" | Keep claims practical and non-guaranteed; connect to personal analysis and expert interpretation. |
| Parenting and education-direction concern | `/children`, psychology experts, astrology experts | "How do I support this child without projecting my fears?" / "Which study direction may fit my child, and how do I validate what I already sense?" | Use careful, non-labeling language; focus on understanding, support, and confirmation of observed talents rather than deterministic career assignment. |
| Method-aware expert search | expert category routes | "I know the method I want; help me choose a person." | Category pages and blog CTAs should reduce choice anxiety and show trust signals. |
| Method-agnostic help seeking | `/experts` | "I need help, but I do not know which type." | Route to catalog/category chooser, not to one modality too early. |
| Practitioner growth | `/for-astrologers` | "Can I grow my expert practice through Astrogen?" | Treat as B2B/supply-side funnel, separate from consumer SEO segments. |

## Use Rules

- Do not name a segment by a keyword cluster, such as "натальна карта" or "сумісність". Name the human state first.
- Use keyword clusters only as search-demand evidence.
- Use GSC/GA4/CrawlObserver to validate priority, not to invent psychology.
- For new products or uncertain routes, run Product Discovery before expanding segmentation.
- For non-blog pages, agents currently do not have confirmed edit/deploy access. Internal links from pages outside `/blog/` must be routed as a technical task for a human/runtime operator with the required access.
