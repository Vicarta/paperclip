# Phase 50.7: Article Capacity Planning And Refill Liveness

## Goal

Make the daily target of three CMS drafts operationally achievable without
weakening Astrogen's existing editorial policy, diversity rules, quality gates,
or topic ownership checks.

## Diagnosis

The allocator correctly limited `zodiac_compatibility` to one article in a
daily batch, but then represented the two remaining slots as a blocked routine.
The current refill could also remain in one source lane's external cooldown
while other portfolio-track deficits had no live continuation. This converted a
content-capacity shortage into a workflow stop.

## Operational Model

- A daily capacity planner runs before the 10:00 allocator and models the
  current day plus the next two Europe/Kyiv calendar days.
- The planner does not reserve topics, write articles, alter priorities, or
  loosen editorial gates. It records a three-day dispatchability matrix in the
  canonical `next-content-plan` document.
- A healthy buffer has nine lineage-valid, independently dispatchable ready or
  reserved topics across that three-day window, after the current repetitive
  query-family, `zodiac_compatibility`, and audience-interest limits are
  applied.
- A shortage of a family or lane is a capacity deficit. The CMO keeps the
  allocator execution issue in progress with a native monitor and advances the
  next missing source lane on the canonical refill case.
- `external_wait` may represent only the named source lane. It cannot park the
  entire refill while another deficient track has no live continuation or can
  proceed with the approved curriculum/semantic source.

## Non-Goals

- No changes to the 12/5/3/3/2 portfolio targets.
- No changes to one-compatibility-topic-per-day, one-concept curriculum, trend,
  cannibalization, Semantic Core, Winning Structure, CMS, image, or
  publication policies.
- No manual article creation, CMS publication, Telegram proactive watches, or
  changes to the old Paperclip instance.

## Acceptance

1. Capacity planning is a scheduled, CMO-owned native routine with a bounded
   three-day matrix and no article-dispatch side effects.
2. The allocator cannot use `blocked` or `blockedByIssueIds` merely because a
   batch family is exhausted; it has an assignee-owned native monitor instead.
3. One source lane's cooldown cannot halt actionable curriculum, semantic,
   trust, commercial, or other deficient portfolio lanes.
4. Contracts, live routine revisions, CMO instructions, native pipeline stage
   automation, and verification stay synchronized.
