# Phase 50.1: One-Concept Western Astrology Curriculum

## Problem

CMS drafts 137 and 138 passed the article pipeline as beginner explainers while
teaching many astrology concepts at once. Draft 137 introduced aspects, aspect
types, planets, signs, houses, rulers, empty houses, a twelve-house overview and
an interpretation workflow. The checks treated these as bounded supporting
context, so the pipeline validated the wrong learning model.

## Decision

For `western_astrology_learning`, one article introduces exactly one new
astrology concept. This supersedes the earlier one-or-two-concept wording.
Aliases remain one concept; teaching an adjacent term in a heading, definition,
table, list, FAQ, example, comparison or workflow counts as another concept.

## Work

1. Add the canonical 12-node curriculum and prerequisite graph.
2. Extend native transition field requirements with conditional application and
   exact array-length validation.
3. Preserve curriculum metadata from search-demand through topic and article
   breakdown.
4. Enforce stage proofs at strategy, structure review, brief, content
   validation, MC quality, CMS draft and CMO delivery.
5. Mark CMS drafts 137 and 138 publication-blocked for curriculum rework.
6. Sync references, agents and pipelines after a clean backup; verify that a
   two-concept canary is rejected and non-curriculum lanes remain unaffected.

## Acceptance

- `introducedConceptKeys` contains exactly one item for every western-learning
  topic and article.
- The item is the case's `primaryConceptKey` and the canonical curriculum node.
- Dependent nodes are not scheduled before their prerequisite articles are
  published and linked.
- Winning Structure may deepen the one concept through several value units but
  cannot add adjacent concepts as information gain.
- Drafts 137 and 138 are visibly ineligible for publication until reworked.
- No CMS content is published or deleted by this phase.
