# Phase 47: Winning Structure And Evidence-Backed Article Value - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-07-14
**Status:** Ready for execution
**Source:** Owner discussion, Winning Structure MCP integration guide, and supplied content-quality prompts

## Phase Boundary

Upgrade the clean Astrogen native article pipeline so every new or refreshed
article is based on durable ownership/cannibalization evidence and a real
reader-value contribution. Keep CMS draft-only delivery and the Phase 46
failure-isolation model.

## Locked Decisions

- Winning Structure replaces the current standalone `serp_check`; it is not an
  additional freeform research prompt.
- The MCP lifecycle uses all five operations and resumes one stored `run_id`.
- A paused run blocks only its article case. Unrelated topic and growth cases continue.
- Reader value is evidence or a concrete deliverable commitment, never LLM invention.
- Astrogen value generation uses article classification and two to four
  type-appropriate value units, not universal blocks.
- Final MC quality is checked after humanization and before layout.
- AI-detector percentages and recursive humanization are forbidden.
- Trend Discovery and scaled-content review are separate periodic workflows.
- GEO/retrievability checks are selective, not mandatory keyword-density gates.
- Canary work remains draft-only and must not regenerate an accepted image for a refresh.

## Astrogen Niche Constraints

- Supported content includes astrology, tarot, numerology, Human Design,
  karmology, consultations, products, relationships, and life decisions.
- Interpretive systems must not be presented as scientific proof or guaranteed prediction.
- Medical, legal, financial, diagnostic, deterministic, and fatalistic claims are prohibited.
- Expert observations, consultation patterns, cases, history, and statistics must
  have provenance, privacy boundaries, and review status.

## Deferred

- Interactive frontend widgets inside articles.
- Automatic CMS publication.
- Rewriting the whole historical blog in one batch.
- Treating trend hypotheses as automatically approved content topics.
