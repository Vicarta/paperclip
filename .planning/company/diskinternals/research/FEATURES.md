# Research: Growth OS Features

## Table Stakes

| Feature | Why It Matters | Phase |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| Company guardrails | Prevents accidental Astrogen operations. | 1 |
| Agent role normalization | Prevents duplicate blog/MKT/SOC ownership. | 1 |
| BigQuery report/plugin contracts | Gives agents stable data inputs without direct GA4/GSC or arbitrary SQL access. | 2 |
| Attribution QA | Prevents optimizing downloads or traffic as if they were sales. | 2 |
| Product and URL mapping | Enables product-level and page-level decisions. | 3 |
| Scoring formulas | Converts data into ranked backlog. | 3 |
| Pilot backlog | Focuses work on confirmed high-value lanes. | 4 |
| PR and approval workflow | Lets agents prepare real changes without autonomous publishing risk. | 5 |
| QA/compliance | Prevents unsafe recovery claims and wrong product routing. | 5 |
| Operating routines | Turns one-time setup into recurring growth operations. | 6 |

## Differentiators

| Feature | Why It Matters | Phase |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| Linux Reader/Linux Writer funnel | Converts utility intent into either low-price writer purchase or relevant recovery product path. | 3-4 |
| Scenario-based popup strategy | Reduces uncertainty instead of pushing generic discounts. | 4 |
| BigQuery growth automation | Makes agents operate from durable marts instead of repeated ad hoc analysis. | 7 |
| Guided product selector | Safer pre-sales routing before AI assistant. | 8 |

## Anti-Features

| Anti-Feature | Risk |
|--------------|------|
| Mass blog generation | Creates volume without revenue attribution. |
| MKT-owned blog workflow | Duplicates SEO and confuses accountability. |
| Immediate AI assistant | High risk of wrong recovery advice or false confidence. |
| Blanket localization | Wastes effort where country traffic has weak purchase/download signal. |
| Bulk reindexing | Burns effort on low-value changes and creates noisy follow-up. |
| Generic sale popup scaling | May increase closes/friction without product-specific conversion lift. |
