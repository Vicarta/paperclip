# 12-01: DiskInternals Paperclip Human User Guide

## Goal

Create a detailed Ukrainian guide that lets a non-technical DiskInternals operator understand how to use Paperclip as a business growth operating system.

## Tasks

<task id="1" type="content-architecture">
<action>Structure the guide around business outcomes first: traffic growth, trial downloads, order-page visits, purchases, product/page priorities, SEO refresh, CRO, localization, and human implementation.</action>
<done>The guide opens with DiskInternals growth outcomes and describes Paperclip as a business operating system before listing roles or tools.</done>
</task>

<task id="2" type="process-documentation">
<action>Explain leadership levels and agent groups as practical business functions, then map them to configured Paperclip workflows.</action>
<done>Leadership and specialist sections explain business responsibility, practical task types, and expected outputs.</done>
</task>

<task id="3" type="operator-instructions">
<action>Document how a human should create useful Paperclip tasks: what business context to include, which outputs to expect, where approval gates are, and how Perfex handoff fits.</action>
<done>The guide includes task templates, good and weak examples, completion expectations, approval gates, and Perfex handoff semantics.</done>
</task>

<task id="4" type="governance">
<action>Describe available plugins as business capabilities and avoid unnecessary internal implementation details or references to unrelated companies.</action>
<done>The plugin section describes business use cases and the guide was checked to avoid unrelated-company references.</done>
</task>

## Verification

- The guide exists at the deliverable path.
- The guide starts from business tasks and explains configured processes after the business need.
- The guide does not mention unrelated companies.
- The guide is detailed enough for a new operator to understand roles, workflows, plugins, approvals, and result review.
